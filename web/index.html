A few things before you plug in.

01 / BROWSER

Desktop Chrome or Edge

The installer needs USB and MIDI permissions. Your browser will ask you to choose your NanoCore and may ask again when it enters update mode.

02 / COMPATIBILITY

Verified firmware 1.10

The installer checks the firmware on your device before making changes. Unsupported images are rejected. This is an independent, experimental modification. The firmware has run on hardware; this browser uploader still needs its first physical-device test.

03 / BACKUP

Keep a copy. Come back anytime.

A backup is saved locally before installation. Download it somewhere safe. Use Restore original to return to your saved firmware.
