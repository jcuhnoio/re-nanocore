Tool scope
The included uploader enforces the contemporary NanoCore structure/identity,
reports the footer digest match, and preserves the original inner signature.
It deliberately does not validate publisher signatures or require the digest
to match. Its inspect command is structural inspection, not authentication.
Legacy1.04 product0/route metadata is unsupported by this uploader.
