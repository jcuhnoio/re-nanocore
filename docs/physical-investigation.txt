Implications and remaining non-invasive work
No executable plaintext, CPU identity, decryption key or arbitrary-address
read primitive was recovered. Review of existing diagnostic research still
finds other-product restrictions, not a NanoCore general flash reader.
The companion JimCommanD source primarily targets Mosaic; do not transfer
its commands or hardware assumptions to NanoCore without tracing dispatch.

Next priority: trace NanoCore-specific normal-mode dispatch for identity,
asset-read and diagnostic commands before sending additional requests. Do not
scan numeric opcodes: known neighboring operations include saves and resets.
Asset reads could supply validated data for package comparisons, but do not
in themselves expose program flash. Determine bootloader exit/recovery behavior
before a separate DFU-only inspection. The current runtime descriptor cannot
answer whether bootloader UPLOAD/readback exists. Do not launch API17 merely
to inspect it: that route transitions and immediately begins firmware upload.
Preserve current 1.10 for exact matching to the local signed 1.10 package.
