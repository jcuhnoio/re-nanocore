The 1.2 notes claim:
- A 48 kHz engine and corresponding USB recording/playback changes.
- Stereo processing throughout the effects chain.
- A left/right ping-pong option for every delay algorithm.
- NAM A2 Lite amp inference at native 48 kHz. This is narrower than arbitrary
  NAM compatibility; the notes do not promise A1, A2 Full or arbitrary networks.
- Ten guitar-driven synth voices, enabled in Settings and stored per preset.
- Peak Comp, Parallel Comp, Classic Wah, Retro Wah and Bass Wah in FX1 and FX2.

The latest listed desktop editor is ToneCommand 1.3.5, whose notes explicitly
add NanoCore/Mosaic 1.2 support. The local inspected app's Info.plist reports
1.3.3 build49; it was not launched or updated during this investigation.

Independent upstream architecture reference
-------------------------------------------
https://github.com/tone-3000/embedded

TONE3000's A2 reference describes A2 Lite as a fixed 3-channel WaveNet with
23 layers, 1,871 float32 weights, kernel widths 6 and15, dilations through239,
LeakyReLU slope0.01 and a16-tap output head at48kHz. These are useful binary
fingerprints. They are not evidence that Livtra uses that repository's code.
Its ARM hardware benchmarks cannot establish NanoCore/RISC-V performance.
