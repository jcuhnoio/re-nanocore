Four-control UniVibe revision, NanoCore1.10
=========================================

Requested interface: MXR M68 style LEVEL, SPEED, DEPTH, CHORUS/VIBRATO.
On-device order is SPEED, DEPTH, LEVEL, MODE. Only four parameters are exposed.
No adjustable WAVE, VOICE or MIX remains. MODE uses the native enum mechanism;
its stored endpoints are0/1, renderedCHORUS/VIBRATO. SPEED/DEPTH/LEVEL display
normalized0..100 values, not a falsely calibrated analog scale or Hertz readout.

Control mapping (our implementation, not claimed MXR pot calibration)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
SPEED: .2+5.8*x*x Hz; default35 (~.91Hz).
DEPTH: modulation excursion0..1; default80.
LEVEL: linear0..2 output gain;50=unity multiplier,100=+6.02dB; default50.
MODE:0=CHORUS (equal dry/wet),1=VIBRATO (wet-only); defaultCHORUS.
Mode changes crossfade briefly to avoid a hard audio discontinuity; that is not
an additional continuously adjustable MIX control.

Official interface reference:
https://www.jimdunlop.com/content/manuals/M68.pdf
The manual documents these control roles and dry-plus-wet versus wet-only modes.
It does not establish our numerical knob mapping or prove sonic equivalence.

DSP revision
------------
Source firmware/custom-fx/src/univibe.c retains the dedicated nine-argument
Velvet ABI. It uses116bytes of existing substate; the native reset at+4 clears
its marker. No heap, globals requiring initialization, or external DSP calls.

The former ideal allpass network is replaced with four non-ideal first-order
phase stages derived from Darabundit, Wedelich and Bischoff, DAFx2019 paper31:
https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/2019/DAFx2019_paper_31.pdf
Original schematic cross-check:
https://experimentalistsanonymous.com/diy/Schematics/Vibrato%20and%20Pitch%20Shift/Univibe.pdf

Fixed Cp=[15n,220n,470n,4.7n]F, couplingC=1uF, seriesR=4.7k.
The schematic says470N; paperTable1 prints470p, an apparent publication typo.
Alpha=[1.01,.98,.97,.95], beta=[1.11,1.09,1.10,1.09] are measurements of one
vintage unit. They are not verified MXR M68 component or transfer measurements.
For eachstage: kc=Cp/(Cp+Cdc),ke=Cdc/(Cp+Cdc),w=(Cp+Cdc)/(Rprime*Cp*Cdc),
A=alpha*ke-beta*kc. H(s)=(A*w-beta*s)/(s+w). Ordinary bilinear substitution
withK=2Fs givesb0=(A*w-beta*K)/(K+w),b1=(A*w+beta*K)/(K+w),a1=(w-K)/(K+w).
This includes frequency-dependent shelving omitted by the previous ideal stages.
The dryfeed approximates the first splitter's emitter,1.01*input, before itsRC
network; it is not the first filter output.

Explicit approximation boundary
-------------------------------
The rounded symmetric triangle LFO,20/60ms lamp smoothing and fourth-power
mapping between measured LDR resistance extrema are our approximations.
Measured per-cell3D optical curves were not obtained. Mixer amplitudes are
normalized for this implementation rather than exact analog resistor loading.
Preamp/buffer saturation is not modeled. Fixed values here mean fixed internal
voicing; they do not establish a faithful, calibrated MXR circuit recreation.
The physical M68 or reference recordings would be needed for that comparison.
